# Student services and lost ID

## Lost student ID card
Report the loss on UniHive Lost and Found, then visit Student Services on Level 1 with another photo ID.
Temporary access lasts three working days while a replacement is processed.

## Wellbeing
Book a confidential session with Student Wellbeing via the services desk, or email wellbeing@ucl.lk.
Walk-ins are available weekdays 10:00–15:00.

## Onboarding week one
Activate your campus account, update your UniHive profile (faculty, year, programme), and join at least one society interest list.
