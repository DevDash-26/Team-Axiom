# Bookings, dining, and finance

## Book a classroom or sports facility
Use UniHive room booking, choose a resource, and wait for admin approval. Same-day sports bookings close at 12:00.

## Dining
The main cafeteria and Block B café accept meal cards. The rooftop kiosk is cash or card only.

## Financial aid
Submit the finance aid form with income documents before the published deadline.
Finance Office reviews applications within ten working days.
