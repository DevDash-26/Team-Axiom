# Campus Wi-Fi and IT

## Reset Wi-Fi password
Visit the IT helpdesk on Level 2 or use the self-service portal linked from Campus Services.
Bring your student ID. Lab access issues are handled the same day at the helpdesk.

## Helpdesk hours
IT helpdesk is open weekdays 08:30–16:30. Email it.help@ucl.lk for non-urgent account problems.

## Print and copy
Black-and-white A4 is Rs. 8 per page; colour A4 is Rs. 40. Top up your print balance at the library desk.
