## Priority key

- **P1 (Must):** explicitly stated in the problem statement. Build first, build deeply.
- **P2 (Should):** clearly implied or strongly improves the solution.
- **P3 (Nice):** differentiator / polish. Only after P1 and P2 are stable and tested.

## Status key

- **Done:** works end to end, validated, tested
- **Partial:** works in part (state what is missing)
- **Not done:** not started or dropped (state why)
